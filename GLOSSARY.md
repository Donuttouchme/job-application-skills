# Job application skills

Skills that turn one person's verified background into job applications for the
Swiss market, tuned for a career change into DevOps / Platform engineering.

## Sources of truth

**Profile**:
The current, verified facts about the candidate that any outgoing document may draw on. Only what is true now; no history.
_Avoid_: dossier, CV data, master CV

**Profile history**:
The record of how profile facts were corrected over time, and why. Never read when writing a document.
_Avoid_: changelog, corrections section

**Search constraints**:
The practical limits on which jobs are viable: commute, working hours, licence, health parameters. Read when choosing and writing to employers, never when writing a CV.
_Avoid_: logistics, preferences

**Never-claim rule**:
A one-line standing ban on a specific inflated claim that was once made and corrected.
_Avoid_: correction, do-not-mention

## Evidence

**Evidence class**:
How much proof the profile holds for a requirement: strong, transferable, weak, or missing.

**Transferable evidence**:
Proven work in another domain that served the same purpose the requirement asks for. Counts half, like weak, but outranks it.
_Avoid_: related experience, soft match

**In-progress item**:
A certification or project that is planned or under way. Never evidence; may be stated only with its date and status.
_Avoid_: upcoming skill, pending evidence

**Command**:
What the candidate could defend in an interview: defend, discuss, or exposure. Independent of evidence class.

**Trace**:
The link from one factual line of an outgoing document to the exact source sentence it rests on. A factual line without a trace is unsourced; a trace alone does not prove the line is accurate.
_Avoid_: source, citation

## Search

**Lane**:
A kind of role the scout searches for. DevOps / Platform is the primary lane; junior software engineer is the second lane, shortlisted only when the primary lane has too few good postings.
_Avoid_: track, category, fallback

## Documents

**Role line**:
The positioning line under the candidate's name on a CV. A positioning, not a title claim.
_Avoid_: headline, title
